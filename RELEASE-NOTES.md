# Cleo 1.0.0 — macOS preview

A tiny desktop goldendoodle who keeps you company in a screen corner. Pet her with a click, play keep-away with a stick, watch her catch a thrown ball, or let her curl up for the evening.

Download `Cleo-1.0.0-macOS-universal-preview.zip`, extract it, and drag `Cleo.app` into Applications. Requires macOS 14 or later on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac. Her controls are in the paw menu in the menu bar.

Cleo runs locally without accounts or network access and does not ask for Accessibility, Screen Recording, or Input Monitoring permissions. She does not start at login automatically.

This is an ad hoc signed preview, not a Developer ID signed, notarized release. macOS may block its first launch. If you trust this download, attempt to open Cleo, then use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, followed by **Open**. [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445) explain the process. Do not disable macOS security settings.

The download includes simple installation instructions. A SHA-256 checksum and version/architecture metadata accompany it. Both executable slices have run on Apple silicon, including the Intel slice under Rosetta; an actual Intel Mac and macOS 14 remain unverified.
